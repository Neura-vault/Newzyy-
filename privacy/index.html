Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Newzyy, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines what information we collect and how we use it — transparently and without legal jargon.

Information We Collect

  • Preferences: Your dark/light mode selection and login status are stored locally to enhance your browsing experience.
  • Analytics: We track aggregate article view counts to understand readership trends. This data is anonymous and not linked to individual users.
  • Communications: If you contact us via email or our contact form, we retain your message and address solely to respond to your inquiry.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties.
  • We do not employ third-party tracking scripts or behavioral advertising.
  • We do not require user accounts to access any article on our site.

Data Sources

Newzyy aggregates factual information from established wire services and public sources to produce original reporting. This process involves one-way data pulls for content creation — no reader data is shared with these external services.

Your Rights & Choices

You may clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove saved preferences. If you wish to request deletion of any personal data we may hold, please reach out via our contact page.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be announced on our homepage. The effective date will always be noted at the top of this page.
