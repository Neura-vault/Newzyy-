St Helens playoff hopes hang by a thread after thrashing by Leeds

By Newzyy Staff — 3h ago

St Helens’ 32-year streak of reaching the Super League playoffs is under severe threat after a heavy defeat to Leeds Rhinos, which all but ended their hopes of a post-season berth this year.

The Rhinos, now firm favourites to finish top of the table, secured a commanding 32-6 victory that leaves their rivals facing an uphill battle to qualify for the playoffs. Leeds’ win, combined with their earlier strong performances, has put them in pole position for the League Leaders’ Shield and a potential semi-final bye.

For St Helens, the loss means their 60-year playoff run is likely over unless a dramatic turnaround occurs in the final two games. Even then, they would need to win both matches convincingly while other teams falter—a tall order given their recent struggles. Their interim coach admitted disappointment, acknowledging that despite an early competitive start, missed opportunities allowed Leeds to dominate.

Leeds’ victory was built on a commanding first-half display, with Jake Connor starring once again. The Rhinos led 18-0 at the break, extending their advantage through further tries before the Saints could muster any response. The result leaves St Helens needing a near-perfect end to the season just to stay in playoff contention.

The Rhinos now turn their attention to a crucial clash with Wigan next weekend, a match that could decide the regular season’s top spot. With momentum firmly on their side, Leeds appear to be pulling away from the pack, while St Helens face a long offseason after another disappointing campaign.
