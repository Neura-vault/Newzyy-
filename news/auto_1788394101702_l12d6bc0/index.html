Bank of England chief warns new AI models threaten global financial stability

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey has raised concerns about the potential risks posed by advanced artificial intelligence models to the stability of the global financial system. In a recent statement, Bailey emphasized that cutting-edge AI technologies could significantly heighten cyber threats, which may undermine the security and resilience of financial institutions worldwide.

According to Bailey, the rapid evolution of AI systems introduces new vulnerabilities that malicious actors could exploit, potentially leading to disruptions in financial markets. While AI offers efficiency and innovation benefits, its integration into critical financial infrastructure also creates potential entry points for cyberattacks. The Governor’s remarks highlight the need for heightened vigilance and robust safeguards to mitigate these emerging risks.

The warning comes amid growing recognition of AI’s dual role in both enhancing and complicating financial sector security. Regulators and financial institutions are increasingly focused on developing strategies to address the challenges posed by frontier AI, including enhanced monitoring, stricter controls, and cross-sector collaboration. Bailey’s comments underscore the urgency of proactive measures to ensure the stability of the global financial system in an era of rapid technological advancement.
