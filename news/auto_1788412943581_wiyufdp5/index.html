B&Q and Five Guys among firms that paid staff below minimum wage

By Newzyy Staff — 56m ago

The government has revealed that over 600 companies, including well-known brands, have been found to have underpaid their staff, resulting in a significant impact on workers' earnings.

The scale of the issue is highlighted by the £4 million that has been returned to employees as a result of these underpayments.

Among the companies named are B&Q, the popular home improvement retailer, and Five Guys, the renowned burger chain.

These businesses, along with many others, have now been instructed to rectify the situation and ensure compliance with minimum wage regulations.

The government's findings shed light on the prevalence of such practices, raising concerns about the financial well-being of workers across various industries.

While the exact reasons for the underpayments are not yet clear, this revelation underscores the importance of robust enforcement measures to protect the rights and livelihoods of employees.

The affected companies are now facing scrutiny and are expected to take immediate steps to address the issue and prevent future occurrences.

This news serves as a reminder of the ongoing efforts to ensure fair and ethical employment practices, with authorities committed to holding businesses accountable for their treatment of workers.
