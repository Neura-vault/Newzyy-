Lords call for AI 'kill switch' powers in UK

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

Members of the UK Parliament's House of Lords have advocated for the introduction of "kill switch" powers for artificial intelligence systems operating within the United Kingdom.

Backers of this proposal contend that such a capability would establish a critical line of defense. They describe it as a "vital safety net," intended to protect against uncontrolled or "runaway" AI systems. Examples of AI developers whose advanced systems would be subject to such a safeguard include companies like OpenAI and Anthropic.
