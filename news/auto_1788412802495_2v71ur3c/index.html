Burnham pressed on economy at first PMQs as borrowing costs surge

By Newzyy Staff — 59m ago

Prime Minister Winston Burnham used his inaugural Prime Minister’s Questions to address the country’s escalating borrowing costs, emphasizing that his administration would remain grounded in fiscal responsibility.

During the session, Burnham confronted Opposition Finance Secretary Kemi Badenoch on the urgent need to curb rising debt service expenses. He reiterated that the government’s policy framework would be anchored in sound fiscal management, even as interest rates continue to climb.

The Prime Minister’s remarks came amid growing concern over the sustainability of public finances, with economists warning that higher borrowing costs could strain government budgets. By underscoring fiscal prudence, Burnham aimed to reassure investors and the public that his administration would take measures to protect the nation’s economic stability.
