Blackout risk rises as heatwave stresses largest US electric grids

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

The U.S. Department of Energy has issued a warning about an increased risk of power outages as extreme heat strains the country’s largest electric grid.

The PJM Interconnection, which operates the grid serving 67 million people across a wide region from Washington, D.C., to Chicago, is expected to face heightened pressure on Wednesday. The system’s ability to meet surging electricity demand during the heatwave is a growing concern.

Officials have highlighted the strain on energy supplies as temperatures rise, raising the possibility of localized or widespread blackouts. The PJM Interconnection manages power distribution for a vast area, including major metropolitan centers and industrial hubs.

The warning underscores the challenges of maintaining grid reliability amid prolonged extreme weather. Energy providers and grid operators are closely monitoring conditions to prevent disruptions, but the risk remains elevated as demand for cooling continues to climb.
