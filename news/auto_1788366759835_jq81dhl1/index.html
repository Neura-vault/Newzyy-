Rupert Murdoch ‘very happy’ that Guy Pearce plays him in Fleet Street biopic Ink

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

Actor Guy Pearce has revealed that media mogul Rupert Murdoch expressed satisfaction with his portrayal in the upcoming biopic *Ink*, though Murdoch had not yet seen the film at the time of the comment.

Speaking at the Venice Film Festival ahead of the premiere of *Ink*—a film about the founding of the British tabloid *The Sun*—Pearce shared that Murdoch’s daughter, Elisabeth, had relayed his father’s approval. “She did say ‘Dad’s very happy that you’re playing him,’” Pearce recounted.

The biopic, directed by Danny Boyle, centers on the early years of *The Sun* and its transformation under Murdoch’s ownership. Pearce, known for his roles in *The Matrix* and *Memento*, portrays the press baron in the film.

Murdoch’s reaction was shared during a promotional event for the movie, which marks Boyle’s return to filmmaking after a hiatus. The film’s premiere at the festival has drawn attention not only for its subject matter but also for its casting choices.
