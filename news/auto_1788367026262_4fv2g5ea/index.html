Mayday review – Ryan Reynolds and Kenneth Branagh set cold war bromance alight

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

The latest action-comedy from Hollywood pairs two A-list stars in a Cold War-era adventure that blends humor, espionage, and high-octane thrills. Set in the 1980s, the film follows an American fighter pilot who teams up with a retired KGB officer for an unexpected and chaotic alliance.

Ryan Reynolds stars as Troy Kelly, a reckless but charismatic fighter pilot whose unorthodox tactics and quick wit often land him in trouble. When his spy plane is shot down over Soviet territory, he wakes up in the remote cabin of Nikolai Ustinov, a retired KGB agent played by Kenneth Branagh. Ustinov, a secret admirer of American culture, offers Troy a bizarre deal: stay hidden in the cabin and watch *Top Gun* on pirated VHS tapes until the Cold War ends.

The film delivers a mix of slapstick humor and over-the-top action, including cramped close-quarters brawls that showcase Branagh’s surprising martial arts skills. The climax features a chaotic chase sequence through Moscow’s streets, evoking the spirit of classic spy films. Reynolds leans into his signature comedic timing, while Branagh brings a mix of charm and deadpan delivery to his role.

Critics have noted the film’s lighthearted tone, though some may find its premise far-fetched. Still, the chemistry between Reynolds and Branagh keeps the story engaging, blending absurdity with just enough suspense to satisfy fans of action-comedy. Whether it’s a standout performance from Branagh or Reynolds’ trademark humor, the film offers a playful take on Cold War tensions.
