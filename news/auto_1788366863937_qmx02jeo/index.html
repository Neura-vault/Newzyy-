India’s richest man now wants to turn aging computers into AI-ready PCs

By Newzyy Staff — 1h ago

India’s most affluent businessman is spearheading an initiative to repurpose older computers into AI-capable machines through a low-cost subscription service. The project, led by Reliance Industries’ Jio, aims to make artificial intelligence more accessible by transforming outdated hardware into functional AI-ready systems.

Under the proposed model, users can subscribe to a service that upgrades their aging PCs for approximately $11 every two months. This approach targets individuals and organizations with limited budgets, offering a practical solution to bridge the digital divide in AI adoption. The initiative aligns with growing efforts to democratize technology by leveraging existing resources rather than requiring expensive new equipment.

By focusing on software-based enhancements rather than hardware replacements, Jio’s strategy could significantly reduce the financial barrier to AI integration. The company has not yet detailed the specific technical methods used to achieve this transformation, but the affordability suggests a cloud-based or lightweight AI processing model. If successful, the service may appeal to students, small businesses, and educational institutions seeking cost-effective ways to engage with AI tools.

The move reflects broader trends in the tech industry, where companies are exploring innovative ways to extend the lifespan of older devices. As AI applications become more widespread, solutions like this could play a key role in ensuring equitable access to cutting-edge technology. Further announcements are expected regarding the service’s rollout and technical specifications.
