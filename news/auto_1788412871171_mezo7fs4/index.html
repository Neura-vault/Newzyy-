Uber launches UK's first robotaxis with a driver - here's what it's like to ride in one

By Newzyy Staff — 58m ago

Uber, the well‑known ride‑hailing service, has introduced the UK's first robotaxis fleet, marking a significant step in autonomous vehicle deployment. The company announced the launch of 15 self‑driving taxis in the UK market, a move that positions it at the forefront of the nation's autonomous transport initiatives.

While the vehicles operate autonomously, Uber has chosen to keep a safety driver in the cabin of each taxi. The safety driver can intervene if necessary, ensuring passenger safety and providing a human presence during the autonomous operation.

The fleet, comprising 15 units, is expected to operate in select areas of the UK, allowing Uber to test and refine its autonomous technology under real‑world conditions. The introduction of safety drivers underscores the company's commitment to safety while advancing autonomous mobility.

Uber's launch represents a milestone in the UK’s autonomous transport journey, signaling a shift toward more advanced mobility solutions. As the company integrates driver‑assist technology with autonomous systems, it aims to deliver safer, more efficient transport to passengers across the country.
