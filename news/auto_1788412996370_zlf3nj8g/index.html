Widow whose husband died after falling into slurry pit warns of pressure on farmers

By Newzyy Staff — 56m ago

Margaret Owen, who lost her husband in a tragic accident when he fell into a slurry pit on a farm, has voiced concerns about increasing pressure on the farming community in Britain.

Owen told reporters that the number of farm-related fatalities has been on the rise, and she expressed worry that the situation is becoming more hazardous. “Things are getting worse,” she said, reflecting on the recent surge in deaths on British farms.

While she did not provide specific statistics, Owen’s comments underscore a growing debate about safety, workload, and the physical demands placed on modern farmers. Her experience highlights the risks that can arise in routine agricultural operations and the need for continued attention to worker safety across the sector.
