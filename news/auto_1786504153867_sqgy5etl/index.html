For a proper celebration of the American Revolution, head to the British Library | Brief letters

By Newzyy Staff — 4d ago

The British Library has drawn praise from a reader for its exhibition on the American Revolution, which was described as a more comprehensive alternative to a recent display at the British Museum.

The free exhibition in the Treasures gallery includes several artifacts such as original stamps from the 1765 Stamp Act, letters from individuals involved in the conflict, and maps that showcase key events and locations.

This diverse collection brings the story of the American Revolution up to date by featuring banners used in recent protests against Donald Trump, highlighting the ongoing relevance of the events that took place 250 years ago.

The exhibition appears to have been a more engaging and informative experience for some readers compared to the British Museum's four-item show, which drew criticism earlier this week.

The British Library's Treasures gallery can be visited at no cost, allowing anyone interested in the American Revolution to gain a deeper understanding of this pivotal moment in history.
