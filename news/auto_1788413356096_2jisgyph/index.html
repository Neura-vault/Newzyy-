Will your name be the next UK storm?

By Newzyy Staff — 49m ago

The Met Office and the Met Éireann have published the list of names that will be used to identify significant windstorms and other severe weather events in the United Kingdom for the upcoming year.

Under the naming system, each name on the list is intended to be assigned to a notable weather event that meets predefined criteria, such as causing widespread damage or posing a serious safety risk. The release of the list allows the public, media and emergency services to quickly refer to storms by a memorable name rather than a technical description.

The list is now available on the official websites of the Met Office and Met Éireann. Residents can simply search the list to see whether their own name or a familiar name appears as a potential storm designation for the next year. The decision to name a storm after a particular name is made by meteorologists once a storm event has been identified and the name chosen from the pre‑approved list.

With the naming list out, the public can prepare for the possibility of a storm that may be called by a familiar name. Those who spot their name among the list may find themselves in the news if a storm is later assigned that designation.
